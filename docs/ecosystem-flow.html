On-Chain Architecture

GenomeRegistry Contract

Incognito Action gate: each nullifier_hash maps to exactly one genome CID. Rejects duplicates. World ID circuit verifies Orb status before write. CID = IPFS hash of encrypted SNP file on Filecoin.

RecordVault Contract

Patient pushes encrypted file to IPFS → gets CID → contract stores (CID, timestamp, record_type). Patient's private key never leaves their device. Contract manages a per-patient CID list.

ConsentManager Contract

Patient signs a consent transaction specifying: institution address, data types, study types, expiry. Generates a time-limited access token. Revocable in one tx. Audit trail stored on-chain (21 CFR Part 11 compatible timestamping).

FDA Rules for Patient-Contributed RWD

21 CFR Part 11

Governs electronic records in FDA-regulated research. Requires audit trails, access controls, timestamps, e-signatures. Explicitly approves timestamping for consent versioning. Permissioned blockchain audit trail is architecturally compatible.

FDA RWE Framework (2018)

Mandated by 21st Century Cures Act. Accepts RWE for: (a) new drug indications, (b) post-approval study requirements. Our patient-contributed longitudinal records with genome anchoring are higher quality than claims data from insurers.

CDISC SDTM / ADaM

Mandatory for all FDA NDA/BLA submissions (studies after Dec 2016). Patient data from our platform must be mapped to SDTM format before submission. The institutional layer handles this transformation. EMA: encouraged but not mandated.

IRB / Ethics Approval

Smart contracts cannot replace Institutional Review Board approval. Human subjects research requires ethics committee review before patient recruitment begins. This is a legal requirement across all jurisdictions — plan for it.

EU/Global Requirements

EU CTR 536/2014

Trial must register in WHO ICTRP before trial starts. Data from unregistered trials "shall not be considered" in EMA assessment (Art. 25). Blockchain registration cannot substitute — WHO ICTRP is a named legal requirement.

DARWIN EU

EMA's RWE platform. Common data model across EU data partners. First studies completed. Our platform could feed into DARWIN EU as a data partner — adding blockchain-provenance layer to their infrastructure.

ICH GCP E6(R2/R3)

International baseline standard. Covers audit trails, system-to-system data transfer, risk-based data governance. Applies in US, EU, Canada, Japan, Australia simultaneously. Our blockchain audit trail maps directly to E6 requirements.

What Gets Published On-Chain

Results Hash

Keccak-256 hash of the official ClinicalTrials.gov/EU CTR posting published as an on-chain event. Timestamp + study ID + registry URL. Anyone can verify: this exact result, unmodified, was posted at this time.

Study Metadata

Study type, population (genome-matched criteria), data sources used, institution identifier, approval authority (FDA/EMA/both). Stored as structured data, queryable by patient profiles.

Finding Tags

Machine-readable: gene variants studied, drug names, disease codes (ICD-10), ancestry groups affected. Smart contract routes findings to matching patient profiles automatically.

Findings Flow Back to Patients

Genomic Match Alerts

"Drug X approved for BRCA2 carriers. Your genome has this variant. Study: NCT###, FDA approved [date]. Discuss with your physician." Patient-specific, not generic news.

Risk Score Updates

New population genomics study → patient's polygenic risk scores recalculated. If a large South Asian cohort study discovers a new T2D risk variant, every South Asian patient on the platform gets updated scores.

Pharmacogenomics Updates

New drug interaction data → patient's medication profile updated. "New study: CYP2C9 poor metabolizers have 3x higher bleed risk on warfarin. Your profile: CYP2C9 *2/*2. Alert your cardiologist."

Study Reward Distribution

Smart contract distributes study payment tokens to participating patients. Pro-rated by data contribution quality, record completeness, genome match to study population. Transparent, automated, auditable.

# Healthcare + Genome Records Platform — Research

Status: IN PROGRESS — being populated by research agents (2026-11-06)

## Concept

Patient-controlled digital health records + genome data marketplace using World ID for unique human verification.

### Core Ideas
- World ID Orb-verified humans can contribute genome data once (genome doesn't change) and earn tokens
- Patients compile their own records: bills, X-rays, scan reports, prescriptions — from any hospital visit
- Single platform, properly dated and mapped, with AI-generated summaries
- Private chain + proof of stake for access control
- Patient owns their data: HIPAA/GDPR/DPDP Act 2023 compliant design

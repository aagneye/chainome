# Data Heterogeneity in Health Records

Status: IN PROGRESS — 2026-11-06

## Three Types

### Semantic Heterogeneity
Same condition/drug called different names across hospitals and systems.

### Structural Heterogeneity
- Paper bills (scanned PDFs)
- DICOM images (X-rays, MRI, CT scans)
- HL7/FHIR structured records
- Printed lab reports
- Doctor notes (unstructured text)

### Statistical Heterogeneity
Inconsistent units, date formats, measurement standards across providers.

## Standards
- FHIR R4 — modern standard for structured health data exchange
- HL7 v2/v3 — older, still dominant in India's hospital systems
- SNOMED CT — clinical terminology
- ICD-10 / ICD-11 — diagnosis coding
- DICOM — medical imaging
